# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

## Overview

This application is intended for personal use only. User privacy is respected, and data access and processing are limited to what is necessary to provide the service.

## Information Accessed

With user authorization, the application may access Google Tasks data, including task information and related metadata.

## Use of Information

Information is used solely to fulfill user-authorized requests and support the application's functionality.

User data is not sold or used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

## Data Storage and Sharing

Information may be processed by third-party services only as necessary to fulfill authorized requests.

Authentication credentials are stored securely and are not publicly disclosed. The application does not maintain a separate permanent database of user task information.

## Data Retention and Control

Information is retained only as necessary for the application's operation. Users may revoke access at any time through their Google Account settings.

## Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information against unauthorized access or disclosure.

## Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in data-handling practices.
